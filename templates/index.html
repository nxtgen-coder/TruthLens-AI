Responsible AI & Estimation Disclaimer

Estimates, Not Proof of Truth: TruthLens AI calculates statistical probabilities based on syntax, vocabulary, sentiment, and learned n-gram associations. A high credibility score does not guarantee factual truth, nor does a low credibility score constitute legal or definitive proof of falsehood.

How TruthLens Evaluates Articles:

  1. N-Gram Stylometry: Analyzes unigrams and bigrams using sublinear TF-IDF scaling, identifying distinct patterns of formal reporting versus sensationalist hyperbole.
  2. Linguistic Anomaly Detection: Evaluates punctuation abuse (excessive ! and ?), excessive capitalization, and conspiratorial buzzwords.
  3. Attribution & Citation Presence: Scans for quotations, named agencies (e.g. Reuters, AP, WHO), and authoritative reporting phrasing.
  4. Probability Calibration: Softmax probability output from the logistic classifier is blended with stylistic risk adjustments to form the 0-100 Credibility Score.
